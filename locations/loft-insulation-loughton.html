Addressing Heat Loss in Older Loughton Properties

A common issue in older Loughton homes is poor heat retention. Even when a heating system is working correctly, inadequate loft insulation can make it harder for rooms to maintain a comfortable temperature during colder periods.

Properties built in the 1930s often have loft spaces that have seen multiple alterations over the years. Previous storage use, electrical works and older insulation upgrades can leave uneven coverage across different sections of the loft floor.

By improving insulation levels throughout the loft space, homeowners can often achieve more consistent indoor temperatures while reducing the amount of heat escaping through the roof.

Our Loft Insulation Process in Loughton
