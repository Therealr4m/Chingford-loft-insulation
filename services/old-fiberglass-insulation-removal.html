Why remove old fiberglass insulation?

Clear Out Compressed Material

Old fibreglass can become flattened and uneven after years of storage, foot traffic and general disturbance. Removing it creates a cleaner starting point.

When old loft insulation becomes a problem

We regularly come across older fibreglass insulation that has been in place for many years. It may still be present across the loft, but that does not necessarily mean it is providing consistent coverage.

  • Insulation compressed into thin or flattened sections
  • Large gaps where material has been moved or disturbed
  • Loose fibres and old material around loft access areas
  • Insulation covering areas homeowners need to access
  • Several layers of old material added at different times
  • An outdated installation that makes a proper upgrade difficult

In these situations, removing the old material can be a more practical starting point than simply adding another layer over the top. It gives the loft a clean base and makes it easier to identify where insulation is missing or uneven.

Common questions about old fiberglass insulation removal

How much does it cost to remove old fiberglass insulation?

The cost depends mainly on the size of the loft, how much insulation needs removing and how difficult the access is. We can assess the job and provide a quote based on the actual amount of work involved.

How long does old insulation removal take?

Many domestic lofts can be cleared within a day, although larger lofts or properties with several layers of old insulation can take longer. The amount and condition of the existing material will determine the time required.

Will removing the insulation cause a lot of disruption?

The work is carried out from within the loft, with care taken around the access point and the surrounding property. We remove the old material rather than leaving bags or loose insulation behind in the loft.

Can you remove only part of the old fiberglass insulation?

Yes, depending on the condition and layout of the existing material. We assess what is actually there before deciding whether a partial removal or a more complete clear-out is appropriate.

Will my loft be ready for new insulation afterwards?

Removal gives you a much cleaner starting point for an insulation upgrade. Once the old material is cleared, the loft can be assessed properly before the next insulation system is installed.
