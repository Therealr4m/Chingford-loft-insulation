Insulating lofts to building regulations in Chingford

Many long-term homeowners in Chingford are still relying on loft insulation installed years ago. While it may still be in place, it often falls short of current building regulations and recommended performance levels.

How we upgrade loft insulation to current standards

We begin by assessing your existing insulation. This includes checking depth, gaps, compression and airflow issues.

From there, we determine whether a top-up is sufficient or if certain areas need correcting before new insulation is installed.

The goal is to achieve:

  • Recommended insulation depth (typically around 270mm)
  • Consistent coverage across the entire loft floor
  • Maintained airflow to prevent condensation issues

Everything is installed with long-term performance in mind — not just a quick improvement.

Common questions about upgrading loft insulation

How do I know if my insulation is below regulations?

If your insulation is thin, uneven, or hasn’t been updated in many years, it’s likely below current recommendations. A quick assessment confirms this.

Will everything need to be removed first?

Not always. In many cases, we can build on existing insulation, provided it’s in reasonable condition and laid correctly.

How long does the upgrade take?

Most loft insulation upgrades are completed within a day, depending on access and the condition of the existing insulation.

Is it worth upgrading if I already have insulation?

Yes. Older insulation often underperforms due to insufficient depth or poor coverage. Upgrading brings it in line with modern expectations.
