Where loft draughts commonly come from

Loft draughts are not always caused by one obvious hole. Older Chingford properties can have numerous small openings created around the ceiling and loft structure over many years.

  • Gaps around the loft hatch and access opening
  • Openings around pipes, cables and other services
  • Gaps around ceiling penetrations
  • Small openings around loft structures and fittings
  • Areas where previous work has left gaps or poorly sealed sections

We look at the loft as a whole rather than concentrating on one visible gap. The important thing is to reduce unnecessary air leakage while keeping required ventilation and access arrangements appropriate for the loft.

Semi-Detached Houses

Common questions about loft draught proofing

How much does loft draught proofing cost?

The cost depends mainly on the size and condition of the loft and how many areas need treating. A smaller loft with a few accessible gaps will generally require less work than a larger loft with multiple openings. We can assess the work before providing a quote.

How long does loft draught proofing take?

Many straightforward jobs can be completed within a day. The time required depends on the number of gaps, their accessibility and the condition of the loft.

Will loft draught proofing make my home warmer?

Reducing unwanted air movement can help make rooms feel more comfortable, particularly where noticeable cold draughts are coming from the loft. The result depends on the existing insulation, ventilation and overall condition of the property.

Will you have to remove all my loft insulation?

Not necessarily. Draught proofing can often be carried out around existing insulation. Where access is restricted or an area needs to be inspected properly, we will explain what needs to be moved before carrying out the work.

Can you seal every opening in a loft?

No. Some loft openings are required for ventilation or other practical reasons and should not simply be blocked. We focus on reducing unwanted draughts while leaving necessary ventilation and access arrangements in place.
